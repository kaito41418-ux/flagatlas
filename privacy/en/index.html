1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Flag Atlas is developed by Melencolia and offers flag, capital and geography quizzes, world exploration and collectibles. This policy covers the offline application identified above and the privacy and support pages under kaito41418-ux.github.io/flagatlas/.

Account registration, cloud synchronization, usage analytics, crash reporting and advertising services are not enabled in the current application. The application does not transmit learning records or preferences to developer-operated servers. We do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The application uses browser or WebView local storage on your device to save progress and settings. The following information supports on-device features and is not automatically uploaded by the application.

  • Quiz results, mistakes, learning-path progress and review state
  • Daily challenge results, unlocked collectibles and acquisition dates
  • Unfinished quizzes and answer state, including text entered in fill-in questions
  • Quiz, language, theme, audio, motion and atlas sorting preferences
  • The current tab's background pattern selection, to avoid consecutive repeats

3. Purposes and local retention

Local information is used to resume practice, schedule review, show results and collectibles, and remember settings. It remains until replaced under application storage limits, cleared by you, or removed by your browser or operating system.

The application keeps up to 30 recent quiz records and 90 daily challenge records. These are record counts, not numbers of days. Learning-path progress, mistakes, collectibles and preferences are not automatically deleted under those two limits.

4. Backups and external links

You can export learning records as a JSON file through application settings or choose a backup file to import. Exports include learning records, collectibles, quiz settings and potentially an unfinished quiz. Some device preferences, including language and theme, are not included.

You choose where to save or share a backup. If you choose a cloud drive, email service or another provider, that provider processes the file under its own policy. The application does not automatically send backups to the developer.

Flag, map and audio assets are bundled with the application and do not require online map or flag services. If you open references or other external links, the destination may process your IP address and other connection information under its own privacy policy.

5. Website and third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts this privacy and support website. GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, separately from the application's local learning records. We have not added analytics scripts, tracking pixels, external fonts, advertisements or login forms to this website.

Our public contact address uses Google Gmail. If you email us, Google and your email provider process information needed to deliver and store the message. Website and email providers handle service records, retention and international transfers under their respective policies.

Application data collection through Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics or AdMob is not enabled. Any services enabled later will be described in this policy before activation.

6. Voluntary contact and retention

If you email us, we receive your sending address, message and attachments you choose to provide. We use them to reply, troubleshoot, verify and handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary personal information.

We retain support emails and attachments only for as long as needed to resolve the request, follow up on issues and meet applicable legal obligations. After resolution, when those purposes no longer require the information, we delete it or remove personally identifying details. You may also request earlier deletion; if information must be retained, we will explain the reason and applicable period. Email providers handle their system records and backups under their own policies.

7. Data security

Android 1.9.0 does not request internet permission, disables application auto-backup and restricts its embedded WebView to bundled content. HTTPS is enabled on this support website. These measures reduce unnecessary transfers; they do not mean that local records or exported JSON files are end-to-end encrypted.

Protect your device and backup files. Device security settings, other people's access to your device, and the storage or sharing services you select also affect security. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Access, deletion and privacy requests

You can view progress in the application or export records. To remove Android local data, use system Settings → Apps → Flag Atlas → Storage → Clear data / Clear storage, or uninstall the application. Labels may vary by device.

For the browser version, clear the application's site data through browser settings. Clearing only cached files may not remove saved progress. Delete exported files, recipients' copies and cloud-drive backups separately at their respective destinations. Local deletion cannot be undone without your own backup.

There is currently no cloud account to delete, and the developer cannot remotely delete records on your device. You can use this website's deletion-request page to contact us about information you voluntarily sent or other privacy requests. Sending a request does not mean information has been deleted automatically.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, or restrict or object to certain processing. Contact kaito41418@gmail.com. We will handle and reply to requests according to their scope and any necessary identity verification. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Audience and teenagers' privacy

Flag Atlas is primarily intended for ages 15–22 and is not directed to children under 15. This audience description does not mean you currently need to provide a date of birth or identity document: there is no account registration, and we do not collect user information through analytics or advertising services.

Account and cloud features are still being prepared. Before opening accounts to users aged 15–17, we will establish a separate guardian-consent, withdrawal and data-deletion process and review the requirements of the distribution regions. Cloud sync will remain disabled until the required consent is complete. Planned optional usage analytics will be available only to adults aged 18 or older with separate affirmative consent. None of these features is currently enabled.

Parents or guardians with questions about information their child voluntarily sent us may contact the email address above.

10. International processing

The application does not transmit local learning records to a developer-operated cloud. When you visit this website, open external links or send email, the relevant providers may process connection or message information outside your location. Their privacy policies describe this processing.

Account and cloud services that have not been enabled are not part of the current processing. Before activation, we will explain the providers actually used, processing locations and applicable safeguards.

11. Policy changes

We will update this page and its date when actual features or information handling change. Before enabling accounts, cloud synchronization, usage analytics or advertising, we will disclose the services enabled, information categories, purposes, retention and deletion practices, and provide notices and choices required by applicable rules. Using the current offline version does not constitute advance consent to future collection.
