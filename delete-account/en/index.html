Flag Atlas — Account & data deletion

Request deletion of your Flag Atlas account or personal data by contacting the developer, Melencolia.

The current public version saves progress on your device. Accounts and cloud sync are not yet available. To remove local progress now, follow the device instructions below.

Submit a deletion request

  1. Email kaito41418@gmail.com, preferably from the email address registered to the account.
  2. Use the subject “Flag Atlas — account and data deletion request”. Specify whether you want your account and associated data deleted, or only support correspondence.
  3. If you are a parent or legal guardian, say so. We will explain the necessary identity and authority checks in our reply.

Open email to request deletion

This button opens your email application. You still need to send the email. Visiting this page does not submit a request or delete anything. If no email application opens, copy the address above into your email service.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details or identity documents in your initial email. We will reply if further verification is necessary. If you cannot access the registered email, explain that in your request.

What the request covers

For an account that exists in a version with cloud services, an account-deletion request covers the login account, cloud learning progress, optional usage records and associated guardian-consent records. Exported backups and local guest progress must be removed separately.

Requests are reviewed by the developer. We will confirm completion or explain any information that must be retained and why. A sent email is not an instant deletion confirmation. This page is a contact-based request route; automated cloud deletion is not yet connected.

Remove local progress

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Flag Atlas → Storage → Clear data / Clear storage, or uninstall the application.
  • Web browser: clear the site data for the website where you use Flag Atlas. Clearing only cached files may not remove saved progress.
  • Delete exported JSON backups and copies in cloud drives or shared folders separately.

Local deletion cannot be undone without your own backup. Clearing device data does not send an account-deletion request.

Website privacy

This page has no analytics, advertising, login form or tracking SDK. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process visitor IP addresses for security. Sending email shares the information you include with Melencolia and the email service providers.
